01 / Project summary

Research problem

Predict RNA m6A modification sites from sequence and sequencing data while accounting for read-count variation.

Method

Reproduce iM6A and m6A-IIN, then explore ConvNeXt/Transformer encoders and a Beta-Binomial objective.

My contribution

Preprocessing, input validation, model reproduction, training and evaluation, HPC execution and visualisation.
