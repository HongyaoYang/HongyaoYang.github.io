Brain MRI Anomaly Detection

01 / Project summary

Research problem

Detect abnormalities in multimodal brain MRI without relying on pathology-specific segmentation models.

Method

Diffusion-based pseudo-healthy reconstruction across T1, T2 and FLAIR, followed by residual and uncertainty analysis.

My contribution

MRI preprocessing, model reproduction and training, Dice/AUPRC evaluation, visualisation and failure-case analysis.
